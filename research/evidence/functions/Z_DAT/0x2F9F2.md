## 2. 不看 C++ 的独立汇编恢复

```text
first_picture = first_start
second_picture = second_start
for counter=first_start; counter<=first_end; counter+=2:
  capture pre-frame BIOS tick
  first_event==-1 ? word_C0BF8=first_picture
                  : sub_2D841(-2,first_event,-2,-2,-2,-2,-2,p1,p1,p1,-2,-2,-2)
  second_event==-1 ? word_C0BF8=second_picture
                   : sub_2D841(-2,second_event,-2,-2,-2,-2,-2,p2,p2,p2,-2,-2,-2)
  first_picture += 2
  second_picture += 2
  sub_2D653
  sub_3DB83(50)  // 2 ticks
  wait until current tick differs from captured pre-frame tick
return 1
```

范围仅由 first_start/first_end 控制；第二序列没有独立 end 比较。两事件均为 -1 时，第二次玩家帧写覆盖第一次。事件分支每帧同时写 current/end/begin picture。
